    How to play

    A random character from the Harry Potter books has been chosen. Guess anyone, and each column tells you how close you are. Play as many rounds as you like.

    • Match. That attribute is exactly right.
    • Close. Some overlap: a shared name, affiliation or family, or the same species but a different blood status.
    • Miss. Nothing in common. On a missed first book, ▲ means the answer first appears later and ▼ earlier.

    First book is where a character first appears in person (memories count). Characters who are only ever mentioned use the book they're first named in.

    Tip: in Settings you can pick which books the mystery character can first appear in, so you avoid spoilers for books you haven't read.
